## 1. Beta Zones (Detailed)

### 1.1 Candy Kingdom (beta zone #1)

#### 1.1.1 Overview
- **Biome**: Pastel candy landscape — candy cane trees, gumdrop bushes, frosting rooftops, chocolate rivers.
- **Size**: Medium-large. ~5-8 min to cross on foot, ~2 min with Sugar Rush tier 2+.
- **Population**: ~50-100 Candy People NPCs, ~20-30 Banana Guards, named NPCs (Bubblegum, Peppermint Butler, Starchy, Cinnamon Bun, Tree Trunks visits).
- **Reputation**: Active in beta. Affects guard behavior, vendor prices, NPC dialogue.
- **Day/night cycle**: Full cycle (locked). Affects Vampire racial (heal on night kill), NPC schedules (hybrid — see 1.1.5), guard patrol shifts, ambient lighting.
- **Weather**: Light weather (locked) — occasional candy rain, cosmetic only. No gameplay impact in beta.

#### 1.1.2 Districts / Sub-zones
1. **Candy Castle** — central. Princess Bubblegum's throne room, royal lab, royal tart vault (mini-dungeon entrance), castle gardens.
2. **Candy Town Square** — main hub. Vendors, BMO save terminal, fast-travel shrine (Cosmic Owl statue), quest board, NPC chatter.
3. **Banana Guard Barracks** — east. Guard HQ, captain (bounty poster), armory, dungeon entrance (when caught).
4. **Residential District** — west. Candy People homes (edible), most candy-eating targets here.
5. **Market Street** — south. Food stalls, crafting mats, dye vendor (post-beta), cosmetic vendor (post-beta).
6. **Royal Tart Vault** — under castle. Mini-dungeon (starter arc option). Royal Tart Golem mini-boss.
7. **Candy Kingdom Dungeon** — under barracks. Banana Guard Dungeon (full mini-dungeon, Warden boss).
8. **Candy Kingdom Gates** — north. Locked until starter arc complete. Leads to Grasslands.
9. **Rooftops** — accessible via Sugar Rush parkour. Hidden chests, achievement paths, sniper-style guard-avoidance routes.

#### 1.1.3 Key NPCs
| NPC | Role | Location |
|-----|------|----------|
| Princess Bubblegum | Starter arc giver, royal quests | Candy Castle throne |
| Peppermint Butler | Dark arts hints, secret quests | Castle library |
| Starchy | Vendor, gossip, lore | Market Street |
| Cinnamon Bun | Comic relief, side quest | Town Square |
| Banana Guard Captain | Bounties, dungeon sentencing | Barracks |
| Tree Trunks (visiting) | Apple pie quest, cooking intro | Market Street cart |
| Banana Guards (x20-30) | Patrol, chase, dungeon escort | All districts |

#### 1.1.4 Activities
- Story quests (starter arc + ~5-10 short quests)
- Candy-eating (reputation chaos)
- Banana Guard interactions (bribe, fight, sneak, impersonate, snitch)
- Sugar Rush parkour (rooftops)
- Royal Tart Vault mini-dungeon
- Banana Guard Dungeon (when caught, or repeatable post-escape)
- Bounties (Grasslands mobs, posted by captain)
- Environmental interaction (push carts, break pots, hide in barrels)
- PvP duels (consensual, anywhere in zone)

#### 1.1.5 Fast travel
- Cosmic Owl statue in Town Square → unlocks fast travel to Tree Fort (Grasslands) once gates open.
- No fast travel within Candy Kingdom in beta (encourages exploration + Sugar Rush use).

#### 1.1.6 Reputation states
| Tier | Guard behavior | Vendor prices | NPC dialogue |
|------|---------------|--------------|--------------|
| Beloved | Salute | -20% | Adoring |
| Liked | Friendly | -10% | Warm |
| Neutral | Ignore | Normal | Polite |
| Disliked | Watch | +10% | Cold |
| Wanted | Chase on sight | Refuse | Screaming |
| Hunted | Chase + reinforcements | Refuse | Fleeing |

#### 1.1.7 NPC schedules (hybrid — locked)
- **Guards**: full shift changes — day patrol vs night patrol, different routes, reinforcement behavior at night.
- **Vendors**: stay put during business hours, don't go home (simpler, always available).
- **Citizens**: a few (~20%) go home at night; most stay in squares/social spots for ambient life. Balances immersion with not locking players out of NPCs.
- **Named NPCs** (Bubblegum, Peppermint Butler, etc.): stay at their posts for quest availability.

### 1.2 Grasslands + Tree Fort (beta zone #2)

#### 1.2.1 Overview
- **Biome**: Open green grassland, scattered trees, hills, ruins, multiple sub-areas. Low-pressure zone but larger than originally scoped.
- **Size**: Large — multiple sub-areas (locked). ~15-25 min to fully explore.
- **Population**: Sparse — wild mobs, a few wandering NPCs, Tree Fort as player home.
- **Reputation**: None (no kingdom authority here).
- **Sub-areas (locked — larger Grasslands)**: 5 sub-areas (see 1.2.2).

#### 1.2.2 Points of interest (expanded for larger Grasslands)
1. **Tree Fort** — player home. Enter, light decor in beta + **2-3 upgrade rooms** (mid-tier beta depth; full tycoon post-beta). BMO save terminal inside.
2. **Grasslands Camp** — central hub. Bounty turn-in, basic vendor, fast-travel shrine.
3. **Old Road** — connects Candy Kingdom gates to deeper Grasslands. Mob spawns along it.
4. **Hilltop Shrine** — fast-travel point (Cosmic Owl statue), viewpoints.
5. **Ruined Outpost** (sub-area: East Grasslands) — small PvE dungeon (optional, repeatable). Bandit camp.
6. **River Crossing** (sub-area: South Grasslands) — scenic spot, **basic fishing spot (locked — 1 spot, simple fishing minigame)**, wild mobs.
7. **Wild Berry Wolf Den** (sub-area: North Grasslands) — mini-boss area, pack of wolves, bounty target.
8. **Whispering Oak Grove** (sub-area: West Grasslands) — atmospheric forest, lore POIs, rare mob spawns, hidden chests.
9. **Canyon Pass** (sub-area: Far East) — narrow passage, ambush spot, connects to "preview" area teasing next kingdom (post-beta).
10. **Mushfield** (sub-area: Central) — open field with mushroom mobs, **beginner-friendly sparse density (locked)**, easy grinding spot.

#### 1.2.3 Key NPCs
| NPC | Role | Location |
|-----|------|----------|
| Camp Vendor | Basic supplies, repairs | Grasslands Camp |
| Bounty Officer | Grasslands bounties | Grasslands Camp |
| Wandering Merchant (scheduled) | Common rare goods, every in-game night at camp | Grasslands Camp |
| Rare Goods Merchant (random) | Very rare spawn, top-tier rare items | Random Grasslands location |
| Hermit (Whispering Oak Grove) | Lore, rare side quest | West Grasslands |
| Fisher (River Crossing) | Fishing tutorial, fish vendor | South Grasslands |

#### 1.2.4 Activities
- Bounties (Candy Bandits, Wild Berry Wolves, Sugar Sprouts, Mushroom mobs)
- Tree Fort housing (mid — enter, decor, 2-3 upgrade rooms)
- Open-world PvE (mob grinding, exploration across 5 sub-areas)
- Optional Ruined Outpost mini-dungeon
- Wild Berry Wolf Den mini-boss
- PvP duels (consensual)
- Environmental interaction (push rocks, climb trees, hide in groves)
- Lore POI hunting (Whispering Oak Grove)

#### 1.2.5 Fast travel
- Cosmic Owl statue at Hilltop Shrine ↔ Candy Kingdom Town Square (once unlocked).
- Cosmic Owl statue at Grasslands Camp (second shrine, unlocks after first visit).
- Tree Fort has its own BMO save terminal (no fast-travel cost from fort to shrines).

## 2. Post-Beta Zones (Summary by Season)

> Full detail added as each zone enters production. See `MVP_BETA_SCOPE.md` §5 for season cadence.

### Season 1 — "Foundations"
- **Ice Kingdom** (3rd kingdom): Ice King, penguins, ice caverns, ice magic intro. Cold biome, ice-elemental mobs. Boss: Ice King (friendly duel) + Ice Golem.

### Season 2 — "Courts & Conflict"
- **Fire Kingdom**: Flame Princess, fire magic, magma mines. Fire-elemental. Boss: Flame King trial.
- **Goblin Kingdom**: Xergiok, scrap markets, goblin city. Stealth/chaos zone. Boss: Xergiok.

### Season 3 — "Beyond Ooo"
- **Slime Kingdom**: Slime Princess, sewers, slime wrestling. Slime-elemental. Boss: Slime Queen.
- **Wildberry Kingdom**: Wildberry Princess, vine bridges, berry patches. Plant-elemental. Boss: Wildberry Matriarch.

### Season 4 — "Cosmic Horrors"
- **Cloud Kingdom**: Cloud Princess, floating city, sky race. Wind-elemental. Boss: Storm Lord.
- **Lumpy Space**: LSP, floating dimension, lumpy physics. Boss: Lumpy King.

### Season 5 — "The Vault"
- **Hot Dog Kingdom**: Hot Dog Princess, eating contests. Boss: Hot Dog King.
- **Turtle Kingdom**: Turtle Princess, slow zone, naval access. Boss: Turtle Sage.
- **Lemongrab Earldom**: Lemongrab 1/2/3, lemon people, ear-shaped towers. Boss: Lemongrab (multi-phase).

### Season 6+ — "Eternal Ooo"
- Box Kingdom, Fly Kingdom, Pig Kingdom, Pillow Kingdom, Crystal Kingdom, Mushroom War ruins, Beautopia, Dog Kingdom, Mars, Founders' Island, Cosmic Realm.

### Dimensions (post-beta, multi-season)
- **Nightosphere** (Season 3): Hunson Abadeer, demons. World boss: Hunson.
- **Dead World / Land of the Dead** (Season 3): Death, Margaret. Spirit realm dungeon.
- **Prismo's Time Room** (Season 4): Prismo, Cosmic Owl, multiverse hub.
- **Crystal Citadel** (Season 4): interdimensional prison, raid.
- **City of Thieves** (Season 2): open-world PvP zone, full-loot gold rules.
- **Bad Lands** (Season 2): open-world PvP zone, territory control.
- **Farmworld** (Season 5): alternate dimension event zone.
- **Pillow World** (Season 5): dimension quest zone.
- **GOLB's Domain** (Season 5+): ultimate endgame boss.

## 3. World Map Structure (beta)

```
[Candy Kingdom]  ←gates (locked until starter arc)→  [Grasslands + Tree Fort]
```

- 2 zones, 1 fast-travel connection (Cosmic Owl statues).
- Both zones instanced shards (hybrid server topology — see GMD §1.9).
- Candy Kingdom: ~30-50 players per shard. Grasslands: ~30-50 per shard.
- Dungeons (Royal Tart Vault, Banana Guard Dungeon, Ruined Outpost): 1-4 player instances.
